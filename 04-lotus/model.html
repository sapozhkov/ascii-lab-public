Scientific references

Lotus and its visitors

  • Field observations of insects visiting sacred lotus flowers helped check which visitors were plausible for the scene.

    Li & Huang (2009) · Effective pollinators of Asian sacred lotus (Nelumbo nucifera): contemporary pollinators may not reflect the historical pollination syndrome

  • Records of bumblebee distribution helped check whether bumblebees and sacred lotus occur in the same regions.

    An et al. (2014) · The bumblebees of North China (Apidae, Bombus Latreille)

  • The species profile helped check the round leaves, the position of the stalk at their centre and how the leaves rise above the pond.

    Kew Science · Nelumbo nucifera · Plants of the World Online
